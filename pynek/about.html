About Pynek

A specialist cybersecurity and digital forensics company, registered and operating from Hyderabad, India.

Who we are

Built by practitioners, for organisations that need straight answers

Pynek Technologies Private Limited works at the two ends of the same problem. Before an incident, we test your systems the way an attacker would and help you close what we find. After one, we acquire and examine the evidence properly so that what you present — to a board, a regulator or a court — holds up.

Alongside that, we supply and support the security platforms our own engineers rely on, and we teach the same work to in-house teams who would rather build the capability themselves.

We are deliberately a specialist firm. Every engagement is run by the people who scoped it, and nothing is subcontracted out of the team.

What we stand for

Four commitments we hold to on every engagement

Authorisation first

Written scope, rules of engagement and a named approver before a single packet is sent or a single disk is imaged.

Confidentiality

Client material is handled on a need-to-know basis, held only as long as the engagement requires, and returned or destroyed on request.

Defensible method

Documented acquisition, verified hashes and an unbroken chain of custody — so the finding survives scrutiny, not just review.

Honest scoping

If a piece of work is not something we should be doing, we say so. We would rather decline than deliver something you cannot rely on.
