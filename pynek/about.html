About the company

The people who scope the work are the people who do it

What we do

Two ends of the same problem

Before an incident, we test your systems the way somebody with bad intent would — then hand your engineers a fix they can actually ship. After an incident, we acquire and examine the evidence so that what you put in front of a board, a regulator or a court holds together under challenge.

Alongside both, we supply and support the security platforms our own engineers work with, and we teach the same methods to teams that would rather build the capability in-house than keep buying it.

We stay deliberately small and specialist. Nothing is subcontracted out of the team, and the examiner named on a report is the person who did the examination.

Operating principles

Four things we do not trade away

Authorisation first

A signed scope, rules of engagement and a named approver exist before a single packet is sent or a single disk is imaged. No verbal go-aheads.

Confidentiality

Client material is handled on a need-to-know basis, transferred encrypted, held only as long as the engagement requires, and returned or destroyed on request.

Defensible method

Write-blocked acquisition, verified hash values and a documented chain of custody — so a finding survives cross-examination, not just an internal review.

Honest scoping

If a test would tell you nothing you do not already know, we say so. We would rather lose the engagement than sell you a report you cannot use.

Engagement model

Five phases, every time

Scheduled assessment or 6 AM incident call — the shape of the work does not change.

PHASE 01

Scope and authorisation

Asset inventory, rules of engagement, test windows, out-of-scope systems, escalation contacts, and a named approver on your side.

NDAAuthorisation letter
PHASE 02

Mapping

What exists, what is exposed and what has already leaked — hosts, services, applications, cloud assets, and credentials on paste sites and dark web markets.

OSINTAsset discovery
PHASE 03

Testing or examination

Manual work on top of tooling, against published methodology. Critical findings are escalated the day they are confirmed.

PHASE 04

Reporting

Executive summary, CVSS v3.1 ratings, reproduction steps, captured evidence, prioritised remediation — plus a walkthrough call with the people who have to fix it.
