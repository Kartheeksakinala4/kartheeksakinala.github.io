Audio Forensics

Two questions: has it been edited, and what is being said

Disputed recordings usually arrive with one of two problems. Either a party alleges the audio has been cut, spliced or assembled from separate sessions, or the recording is genuine but the critical passage is buried under traffic, machinery, overlapping speech or handling noise.

Authenticity work examines continuity: the noise floor, spectral signatures at suspected edit points, encoding and container structure, compression history, and where a mains hum is present, electrical network frequency analysis against reference data. Edits leave discontinuities that are difficult to hide from a spectrogram even when they are inaudible.

Enhancement is constrained by physics. We can reduce steady-state noise, attenuate specific interference and improve intelligibility, and we can often lift speech that sounds hopeless on a phone speaker. We cannot create detail that was never captured, and any transcript we produce marks uncertain passages as uncertain rather than guessing.

Coverage

What we examine

Continuity and edits

Spectral and waveform examination for splices, insertions, deletions and overdubs, including edits that are inaudible on playback.

Encoding history

Whether the file has been re-encoded, converted between formats or passed through a messaging platform — each leaves a detectable signature.

ENF analysis

Where a mains hum was captured, comparison of its frequency variation against electrical network reference data to test the claimed recording time.

Speech enhancement

Targeted reduction of traffic, machinery, wind and handling noise to lift intelligibility of the passage in question.

Transcription

Time-coded transcript with speaker turns, and explicit marking of passages that cannot be determined with confidence.

Device consistency

Whether the recording’s characteristics are consistent with the device and application it is claimed to have come from.

Questions

What clients ask first

Can you tell us who is speaking?

Speaker comparison is a specialist discipline with real limitations, and it needs suitable reference samples recorded under comparable conditions. Where the material supports it we can offer an opinion with a stated confidence level. Where it does not — short utterances, heavy noise, mismatched channels — we will say the material does not support a conclusion rather than produce one that will not survive challenge.

It was sent over WhatsApp. Does that matter?

Considerably. Messaging platforms re-encode audio, which discards data and overwrites some of the signatures authenticity analysis relies on. We can still examine it and may still find edits, but the strongest conclusions need the original file from the recording device. Always retrieve that if it still exists.

Will this stand up in court?

The examination is conducted so that it can. Acquisition is write-blocked and hash-verified, custody is documented from receipt to return, the method is recorded in enough detail to be repeated by another examiner, and the report carries a named examiner’s statement. Where an electronic record is to be produced, we supply the certificate required under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (previously Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872). Admissibility is ultimately a matter for the court, and your counsel should review the material before it is filed.
