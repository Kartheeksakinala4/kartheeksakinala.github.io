The practice

Evidence that survives someone trying to discredit it

A forensic finding is only worth what it withstands. That means the original is never examined directly, acquisition is write-blocked and hash-verified, every transfer is recorded, the method is documented in enough detail for another examiner to repeat it, and the report separates what the evidence establishes from what it merely suggests.

It also means being explicit about limits. “Not present in the extraction” and “not present on the device” are different findings. A volume of data leaving the network is not the same as knowing what was in it. Reports that blur those distinctions fail at exactly the moment they are needed.

Where an electronic record is to be produced, we supply the certificate required under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (previously Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872).

Before you call

What to do in the first hour

The single most common reason an examination cannot answer the question is what happened before the evidence reached us.

DO NOT

Power-cycle, reinstall or “clean” the machine

Rebooting destroys volatile memory, which frequently holds the encryption keys, the injected code and the active connections. Reimaging destroys the record entirely. Isolate from the network instead of shutting down.

Isolate, do not wipe
DO NOT

Run recovery tools on a failing drive

Utilities force repeated reads, and repeated reads on a drive with a failing head assembly turn a recoverable fault into scored platters. Power it off and leave it off.

Power off
DO

Preserve logs and footage before they roll

Firewall and cloud sign-in logs commonly retain 30 to 90 days or less; a DVR may overwrite in four. Extend retention, export now, and stop the recorder from overwriting — before anyone decides whether to investigate.

Extend retentionExport now
DO

Start a contemporaneous record

Who noticed what, at what time, who touched which device and what they did. Written at the time, this record is worth more later than anyone expects — and it cannot be reconstructed afterwards.

Write it down now
Questions

What clients ask first

How quickly can you start?

For preservation, immediately — call and we will talk you through isolating the device, extending log retention and stopping a recorder from overwriting, before any paperwork exists. Examination itself begins once we have the media and a written instruction, typically within one to two working days of receipt.

Do you work for individuals, or only companies?

Both. A significant share of matters are individual: a disputed recording, a compromised account, a phone in a family dispute, a drive holding the only copy of something irreplaceable. The method does not change with the size of the client.

What does a forensic examination cost?

It depends on media volume, device type and the question asked — a single phone extraction and a multi-device data theft enquiry are different engagements. We quote against a written scope after understanding what you need established, and for recovery work the assessment comes first so you decide with a realistic likelihood in front of you.

Can you act as an expert witness?

The report is prepared as an examiner’s statement with a named author, and we will attend to explain the method and findings where required. Appointment terms are agreed with your counsel, who should review the material before anything is filed.
