Industries

Industries we serve

The work changes shape by sector — what is being protected, who is asking for evidence, and which regulator is watching.

Manufacturing

Flat plant networks, unpatchable equipment controllers and supplier connections into the corporate estate — usually a segmentation and access problem before anything else.

Banking & Financial Services

Application testing to satisfy customer and audit requirements, payment fraud investigation, and mailbox compromise leading to diverted transfers.

Healthcare & Diagnostics

Patient records across clinical systems and shared drives, imaging equipment on the general network, and DPDP obligations attached to sensitive data.

IT Services & SaaS

Pre-launch application testing, customer security questionnaires, multi-tenant access control, and API surfaces that expanded faster than the review process.

Legal & Advisory

Forensic examination for matters in progress, examiner statements for filing, and protection of client material held under professional obligation.

Education & Research

Open campus networks, large unmanaged device populations, student data under the DPDP Act, and research material worth stealing.

Logistics & Retail

Point-of-sale and warehouse systems, seasonal staff turnover with standing access, and card data handled across a distributed footprint.

Public Sector & NGOs

Constrained budgets, long-lived legacy systems, and citizen or beneficiary data that carries obligations regardless of the size of the IT team.
