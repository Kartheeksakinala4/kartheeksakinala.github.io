How we work

A predictable engagement, every time

No surprises on scope, cost or timing — you know what happens at each stage before we begin.

1

Understand

A confidential conversation about what you have, what you are worried about and what a good outcome looks like.

2

Scope & authorise

Written scope, rules of engagement, timelines and named contacts — signed off before any testing or acquisition.

3

Execute

Our own specialists do the work, keep you updated as we go, and escalate anything critical the same day we find it.

4

Report & support

A report you can act on, a walkthrough with your team, and retesting once the fixes are in place.
