Video Forensics

Most CCTV evidence is lost before anyone looks at it

The common failure is not technical. It is that footage is exported by pointing a phone at a monitor, or the DVR overwrites the relevant window while people decide whether to involve anyone, or a proprietary player refuses to run on any machine in the building and the file is written off as unusable.

We recover footage directly from DVR and NVR storage, including systems using proprietary containers and file systems, and convert it to a standard format without the generational loss that re-recording introduces. Where the recorder has already overwritten, deleted segments can sometimes be carved from the drive.

Examination then covers authenticity — frame continuity, dropped and duplicated frames, encoding history, timestamp consistency against the recorder’s clock — and enhancement of specific frames: stabilisation, super-resolution across frames, de-interlacing and targeted contrast work on a region of interest.

Coverage

What we handle

Proprietary CCTV formats

Recovery and conversion from DVR and NVR systems whose vendor players will not run, or will only run on one machine that no longer exists.

Deleted footage recovery

Carving overwritten or deleted segments from recorder storage, where the overwrite cycle has not yet reached the relevant window.

Authenticity and continuity

Frame-level continuity checks, dropped and duplicated frame detection, splice identification, and encoding history analysis.

Timestamp verification

Whether the burned-in or embedded timestamp is consistent with the recorder’s clock, the file system and the surrounding footage — recorder clocks are wrong more often than not.

Frame enhancement

Stabilisation, multi-frame super-resolution, de-interlacing and region-specific processing to make a plate, face or action legible.

Deepfake indicators

Temporal inconsistency, facial landmark instability, blending boundaries and compression anomalies characteristic of synthesised or face-swapped video.

Questions

What clients ask first

The DVR only has four days of storage and it happened yesterday. What do we do?

Stop the recorder from overwriting immediately — disconnect the cameras or power the unit down — and get the drive to us. Do not export clips through the DVR menu as the only preservation step: menu exports are frequently re-encoded, time-trimmed and stripped of metadata. Imaging the drive preserves everything, including segments the interface will not show you.

Can you enhance a number plate that is a few pixels wide?

Sometimes, within limits that are set by the footage rather than by the software. Where the vehicle appears across many frames, multi-frame techniques can genuinely recover detail that no single frame contains. Where the plate occupies a handful of pixels in one blurred frame, it cannot — and anything that produces a “clear” plate from that is inventing it. We will tell you which case you have after looking at the footage.

Will this stand up in court?

The examination is conducted so that it can. Acquisition is write-blocked and hash-verified, custody is documented from receipt to return, the method is recorded in enough detail to be repeated by another examiner, and the report carries a named examiner’s statement. Where an electronic record is to be produced, we supply the certificate required under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (previously Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872). Admissibility is ultimately a matter for the court, and your counsel should review the material before it is filed.
